Privacy Policy

Information about data, external services and your choices.

Effective date: October 25, 2026

This policy describes how BrowseBloom ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you visit browsebloom.online. This policy should be updated whenever our actual tools or data practices change.

Information we receive

BrowseBloom is an informational website. We do not currently provide user accounts or collect payment details directly. If you email us, we receive the information you choose to share, such as your email address and message, and use it to respond to you.

Website hosting and technical data

Our website is hosted using GitHub Pages. Like other hosting providers, the service may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser information and request logs to deliver and secure the website. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Cookies, advertising and analytics

At the time this policy was prepared, BrowseBloom's supplied site design does not include Google AdSense advertising code, a Google Analytics tag, or a visitor tracking form. If we introduce advertising, analytics or other services that use cookies or similar technologies, we will update this policy and implement any required consent or privacy controls before activating them.

Advertising partners, if added, may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, measure or personalize ads, subject to applicable law and user choices. Google's privacy information is available at How Google uses information from sites or apps.

Affiliate links and external websites

Some articles may contain affiliate links. When you follow a link to a retailer or other third-party website, that site's privacy practices apply. Affiliate networks or retailers may use tracking technologies to attribute qualifying purchases. See our Affiliate Disclosure.

How we use information

We use information sent to us to answer inquiries, review corrections, address legitimate business requests and maintain the website. We do not sell the contents of messages you send us.

Retention and security

We keep correspondence only as reasonably needed for the purpose of the communication and related recordkeeping. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may choose not to contact us or visit external links. You can manage cookies and site permissions through your browser. If you want to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you sent directly to us, please contact us through our Contact page. Rights may vary depending on where you live.

Children's privacy

BrowseBloom is intended for a general audience and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services change. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent using our Contact page.
